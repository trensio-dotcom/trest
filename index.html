About Us

Building Skills.

Trensio is a growth-focused organization dedicated to empowering young people and communities through practical education, digital skills and creative development. We believe that when one person rises, they create a path for others to rise too.

To equip individuals with relevant skills, knowledge and confidence needed to grow, create and succeed in a changing world.
